# Research log — September 2026 re-check

The research table [`nc_data_center_moratoriums.csv`](nc_data_center_moratoriums.csv) is a
snapshot as of **2026-08-29**. This log records a re-check carried out on **2026-09-20**: what
changed, what was confirmed, what is still unresolved, and the source behind each line. The
changes themselves live in [`research_updates.csv`](research_updates.csv), one complete record
per row; the August table is unchanged.

**Method.** Every county was searched for coverage published since mid-August, along with every
jurisdiction that had a vote, hearing or expiration pending. Where a jurisdiction's own records
were reachable they were used; otherwise local news. Specialist newsletters and trackers were
treated as leads, not as evidence — where a claim rests on one of them alone, the record says so
and is outlined in black on the map.
