# Research log — September 2026 re-check

The research table [`nc_data_center_moratoriums.csv`](nc_data_center_moratoriums.csv) is a
snapshot as of **2026-08-29**. This log records a re-check carried out on **2026-09-20**: what
changed, what was confirmed, what is still unresolved, and the source behind each line. The
changes themselves live in [`research_updates.csv`](research_updates.csv), one complete record
per row; the August table is unchanged.

**Method.** Every county was searched for coverage published since mid-August, along with every
jurisdiction that had a vote, hearing or expiration pending. Where a jurisdiction's own records
were reachable they were used; otherwise local news. Specialist newsletters and trackers were
treated as leads, not as evidence — where a claim rests on one of them alone, the record says so
and is outlined in black on the map. A second pass then went back over every weak record to try
to reach a primary source; what that settled, and what it did not, is noted below.

## Still unresolved

- **Perquimans County** — the advertised 2026-09-08 hearing appears to have gone ahead; the
  county has posted no minutes and no outlet covered it. Left as pending and outlined, not
  recorded as adopted or rejected.
- **City of Charlotte** — a nine-month extension cleared committee on 2026-09-08 and WFAE
  reported a council vote set for 2026-09-14. The council's own agenda system records no data
  center or moratorium item among the 70 items on that agenda — whose other items carry
  recorded actions — nor on the 2026-09-21 meetings. So the extension is still pending and the
  printed end date of 2026-11-05 stands for now.
- **City of Lincolnton** — the council postponed its decision on 2026-08-06 and said it would
  resume the hearing in September; no September coverage was found. Still deferred.
- **Town of Bailey** — still rests on a single tracker entry. Not re-examined in this pass.

## Also noted, not recorded

- **Pasquotank County** — the moratorium stands; a final draft UDO presented 2026-09-14 would
  ban data centers outright, but it has not been adopted.
- **Trackers list 2023-era crypto-mining moratoria** in Buncombe, Cherokee, Henderson, Madison
  and McDowell counties, all since expired or replaced. This map covers the 2026 wave, so they
  are not recorded here — a scope question worth settling before the next update.
- **Creedmoor** (Granville) is reported to have adopted data center regulations rather than a
  moratorium. Not verified, so not recorded.
