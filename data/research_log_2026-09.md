# Research log: September 2026 re-check

The research table [`nc_data_center_moratoriums.csv`](nc_data_center_moratoriums.csv) is a
snapshot as of **August 29, 2026**. This log records two later passes: a re-check of every
county on **September 20** and a verification pass on **September 24** that went after
government records for every uncertain entry. The changes themselves live in
[`research_updates.csv`](research_updates.csv), one complete record per row. The August table
is unchanged.

**Method.** Every county was searched for coverage published since mid-August, along with every
jurisdiction that had a vote, hearing or expiration pending. Where a government's own records
were reachable (minutes, agendas, packets, meeting systems) they were used; otherwise local news.
Specialist newsletters and trackers were treated as leads, not evidence. Where a claim still
rests on one of them alone, or on a passing mention with no date, the record says so and is
outlined in black on the map.

## Still unresolved

- **Perquimans County.** The draft ordinance posted with the September 8 packet would impose a
  one-year moratorium from September 8, 2026 to September 7, 2027. The hearing appears to have
  gone ahead, but the county has posted no minutes and no outlet covered it. Left as pending
  and outlined, not recorded as adopted or rejected.
- **City of Charlotte.** A nine-month extension cleared committee on September 8 and WFAE
  reported a council vote set for September 14. The council's own agenda system records no
  data center item among the 70 items on that agenda, whose other items carry recorded actions,
  nor on the September 21 meetings. The extension is still pending, so the printed end date of
  November 5 stands for now.
- **City of Lincolnton.** The council postponed its decision on August 6 and said it would
  resume the hearing in September; no September coverage was found. Still deferred.
